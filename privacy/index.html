Privacy Policy

Tonight We Hold turns your real-world activity into a fantasy kingdom. Everything it needs to do that stays on your iPhone.

The short version: Tonight We Hold has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It reads your step count and workouts from Apple Health on your device and never uploads, sells or shares them.

Who operates Tonight We Hold

Tonight We Hold is provided by Pairbeam (“we,” “us” or “our”). Privacy questions can be sent to berkay@pairbeam.app.

Apple Health data

With your permission, the app reads two kinds of data from Apple Health:

  • Step count, to calculate the day’s Energy; and
  • Workouts (their duration), to add workout Energy.

The app only reads this data. It never writes to or changes Apple Health. Health data is processed on your device only, is never sent to us or anyone else, and is never used for advertising or marketing. You can change Health access at any time in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

Information stored on your device

To keep your kingdom between sessions, the app stores the following locally on your iPhone:

  • your game progress: castle, buildings, commander, gold, experience and raid history;
  • today’s step and workout totals and the Energy earned from them; and
  • your settings, such as language and whether onboarding is complete.

This information is not uploaded or synced to any server we operate.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders on your device, such as a morning scout report and a warning before the nightly raid. No notification content is sent through our servers.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, advertising identifier, device identifiers or usage analytics. The app contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete the app, which removes all of it. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing on our side to delete.

Children

Tonight We Hold is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it handles data, for example by adding optional purchases or crash reporting, we will update this policy before that release. The current version and its date will always be available at this address.
