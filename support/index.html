Support

How can we help?

For help with Tonight We Hold, email berkay@pairbeam.app.

My steps or Energy are not updating

  • Open iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Tonight We Hold and allow Steps and Workouts.
  • Carry your iPhone (or wear your Apple Watch) while you walk.
  • Return to the kingdom and pull down to refresh. Apple Health can take a few minutes to record new steps.

My workouts don’t give Energy

Workouts count when they are saved to Apple Health, whether from Apple Watch or apps such as Strava or Nike Run Club. How much Energy they give depends on your commander: a Ranger earns everything from steps, a Battlemage mostly from workouts.

The raid happened while the app was closed

That’s expected. Raids resolve at 23:00 from the defenses you prepared, and the result appears the next time you open the app. You never need the app open at raid time.

I lost a raid

Losing never resets your kingdom. The wall takes damage and some gold is stolen, but your Builders repair the wall each morning and Reinforce Wall repairs it straight away.

Changing language or commander

Language is in Settings (the gear on the kingdom screen). To change your commander, tap your hero in front of the gate. After your first change you can switch once a week.

Contact

Tell us what happened, your iPhone model, iOS version and the app version shown in Settings. Please don’t send health records or other sensitive information.
